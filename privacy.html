Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Siren Reel Uploader (“the App”) handles information. The App is a desktop application operated by the creator of Siren Reel (“we”, “us”). It runs only on our own computer, and its only user is the owner of the Siren Reel accounts. It is not offered to the public.

2. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Siren Reel YouTube channel. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information the App accesses

Google and YouTube

When the account owner signs in with Google, the App asks for two permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload) – to upload clips to the authorized channel and set their title, description, tags and visibility.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) – to check the status of a video the App has just uploaded, for example whether it is public.

The App does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, watch history or any other data in the Google account, and it does not access data of any other YouTube users.

TikTok

With the account owner’s permission, the App uses TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic) and the TikTok Content Posting API (video.upload) to send clips to the Siren Reel TikTok account as drafts. The account owner finishes and publishes each post in the TikTok app.

Public livestreams

To find highlight moments, the App processes publicly available Grand Theft Auto livestreams on Twitch and Kick: their video, audio and public live chat messages, including the usernames shown publicly in the chat. Speech transcription and the evaluation of clips run locally on our computer. Published clips always name the streamer and may show the public chat as it appeared during the stream.

4. How information is stored

  • Sign-in tokens for YouTube and TikTok are stored only in local files on our computer. We do not run any server. The tokens are never sent anywhere except to Google and TikTok when the App uses their APIs.
  • The App does not keep data obtained from YouTube API Services, except the ID and link of each video it uploads, which are included in the upload confirmation described in section 5.
  • Materials used to evaluate clips (still frames, transcripts and samples of public chat messages) are kept only on our computer and are used only to improve highlight detection.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. Information leaves our computer only:

  • to Google (YouTube) and TikTok, when the App uploads a clip through their APIs;
  • to Telegram, where a private bot sends the account owner each clip for approval and then a confirmation with the link to the uploaded video.

Siren Reel Uploader’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The App does not place cookies or similar technologies on anyone’s device. It stores its sign-in tokens and settings as files on our own computer. This website does not use cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses to keep the service secure – see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the App’s access to their Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. Access to TikTok can be removed in the TikTok app’s security settings, in the list of apps connected to the account. Once access is revoked, the App can no longer use the account, and we delete the stored tokens.

8. Deleting information and removal requests

To have information deleted – for example a stored token, a sample of chat messages, or a clip that features you – email us. We will delete it within 30 days, and usually much sooner. Streamers can ask for any clip to be taken down at any time.

9. Children

The App and the Siren Reel channels are not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
