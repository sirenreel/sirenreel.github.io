Siren Reel Uploader

A private desktop tool that the creator of Siren Reel uses to publish short gaming highlight clips to Siren Reel’s own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What it does

  1. Watches public Grand Theft Auto livestreams and spots highlight moments – for example when the live chat suddenly gets much busier than usual.
  2. Cuts a short vertical clip, adds captions and names the streamer in the title and description. Every clip credits its creator.
  3. Sends each clip to the channel owner for review. Nothing is posted until the owner approves it by hand.
  4. Uploads approved clips to the Siren Reel YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API, and sends them to Siren Reel’s TikTok account as drafts using the TikTok Content Posting API.

Who can use it

Siren Reel Uploader is not a public service. It runs on the channel owner’s own computer and is used only to manage the Siren Reel accounts. There are no sign-ups and no other users.

How the tool handles data is described in the Privacy Policy. Its use is governed by the Terms of Service.
